01, Pure & Organic

Organic.
Nothing else, ever.

Coffee should have nothing to hide, organic beans and nothing else. No pesticides, no synthetic fertilizers, no shortcuts. Just clean caffeine in every cup.

02, Certified & Traceable

Traceable
From green to roast.

Genuinely certified organic, verified, not just labeled. We trace every roasted bean back to the exact green coffee it came from, so you always know where your cup comes from.

03, Precision Roasters

Precision
Roasted to the second.

We don't roast in bulk and hope for the best. Every batch is dialed in to the exact profile of that bean, its origin, its density, its optimal development point.

Pre-2020

A coffee lounge with a live music stage.

Mike and Lisa run a beloved music venue + coffee lounge in Melbourne. Mike is the host, keeping the room running, making sure everyone who walks in feels like a regular by their second visit. Lisa is on a quiet hunt for a coffee good enough to serve their guests.

The Venue Years
2017

Lisa teaches herself to roast.

No organic coffee on the market hits her bar. So she buys a manual drum roaster and stands behind it. Twenty thousand hours later, she has profiles dialed to the second.

20,000 Hours
Late 2019

Christine & Justin come home.

Mike's sister Christine and her husband Justin move back to Florida from Los Angeles. She's an artist building her own body of work. He's spent a decade designing immersive installations for museums. They're figuring out what comes next.

Family Reunion
March 2020

The pandemic answers the question.

Everything locks down. Plans collapse. The four sit across a kitchen table and bet on the coffee. If the world is going to change anyway, they'll build something worth having on the other side.

The World Stops
July 2020

The first cafe opens its doors.

Justin designs the destination, the murals, the room, the world of ACR. Christine builds the brand identity and the logos. Mike & Lisa develop every drink on the menu. The first cafe opens in the middle of a global shutdown. People come back. Then they bring friends.

Opening Day
2023

A second cafe, same obsessive standards.

Demand outgrows the original. The family opens a second destination in West Melbourne. Same beans, same precision, a new corner of the city.

West Melbourne
2026

Built by family. Roasted by humans.

Six years in. Two destination cafes. Twelve roasts shipping nationwide. The roaster is still hand-tuned every morning. The family still bickers. The journey continues.
